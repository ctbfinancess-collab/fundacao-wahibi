Cookie Policy

Cookies are small files a website can store in your browser to remember information. This policy explains which technologies of this kind are used on the Wahibi Foundation website.

Draft document, subject to legal review before official publication.

Cookies currently in use

At present, this website does not set its own cookies and does not use audience analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

Third-party services

The website loads typefaces from Google Fonts. When it does so, your browser communicates with Google servers, which receive the visitor’s IP address. More information is available in Google’s privacy policy.

How to control cookies

You can block or delete cookies at any time in your browser settings. This does not prevent you from browsing this website.

Changes

If the Foundation starts using cookies or analytics tools, this policy will be updated beforehand and, where required, your consent will be requested.
