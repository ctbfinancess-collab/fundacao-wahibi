Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the Wahibi Foundation processes the personal data of people who visit this website, contact us, subscribe to our news or make donations, in accordance with the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (Law No. 13,709/2018 – LGPD).

Draft document, subject to legal review before official publication.

1. Data controller

Fundação Wahibi para Inovação e Desenvolvimento, CNPJ 60.512.994/0001-57, SHCN Quadra 102, Bloco A, Sala 102, Asa Norte – Brasília/DF, 70722-510, Brazil. Contact: contato@fundacaowahibi.org.

  • Data Protection Officer (DPO) and dedicated channelTo be confirmed

2. Data we collect

  • Contact and partnership forms: first name, last name, email and the message sent.
  • News subscription: email address.
  • PIX donations: the payer identification data that the financial system makes available to the recipient (for example, name and originating institution).
  • Browsing: IP address and technical browser data, processed by the services required for the website to work (see item 5).

3. How we use data

  • To answer messages, requests and partnership proposals.
  • To send news about projects and initiatives to those who subscribed.
  • To record and reconcile donations and account for the use of resources.
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations.

Processing relies on the legal bases provided for in the LGPD, such as consent (news subscription), responding to requests made by the data subject and compliance with legal obligations.

4. Sharing

The Foundation does not sell personal data. Data may be processed by service providers that enable the website, message delivery and donations, only to the extent necessary for these purposes, or shared when required by law or a competent authority.

Providers currently used: GitHub (website hosting), Railway (server that processes the forms) and Resend (delivery of form messages to the Foundation by email). These services may store data outside Brazil.

5. Third-party services and international transfer

To display its typefaces, the website loads files from Google Fonts, which means the visitor’s IP address is received by Google, including on servers outside Brazil. PIX donations are processed by the financial institutions of the donor and of the Foundation.

6. How long we keep data

Data is kept for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described, or for the period required by law. News subscriptions are kept until the data subject asks to unsubscribe.

7. Your rights

Under article 18 of the LGPD, you may request at any time:

  • confirmation that processing takes place and access to your data;
  • correction of incomplete, inaccurate or outdated data;
  • anonymisation, blocking or deletion of unnecessary data or data processed unlawfully;
  • data portability;
  • information about whom your data has been shared with;
  • withdrawal of consent and deletion of data processed on that basis.

8. Security

We adopt reasonable technical and administrative measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access and accidental or unlawful situations.

9. Children and adolescents

This website is not intended to collect data from children. Data of children and adolescents involved in projects is processed in their best interest and with the consent of a parent or guardian where required.

10. How to contact us

To exercise your rights or ask questions about this policy, write to contato@fundacaowahibi.org.

11. Changes

This policy may be updated. The current version will always be available on this page.
