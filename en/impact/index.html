Our Impact

Transforming lives is our purpose. Building a better future is our commitment.

At the Wahibi Foundation, we believe that true social impact goes beyond numbers. It is revealed in the opportunities created, the knowledge shared, the communities strengthened and the lives that can be transformed.

Our work seeks to connect people, institutions and partners around initiatives that promote dignity, inclusion, innovation and sustainable development.

Our Commitment by the Numbers

  • People and Communities

    Every person matters. Our commitment is to help expand opportunities and improve the quality of life of communities.

  • Projects and Initiatives

    We turn purpose into structured proposals, seeking solutions that respond to social, educational, cultural and environmental challenges.

  • International Connections

    We believe in cooperation between countries, cultures and institutions as a way to share knowledge and extend the reach of social initiatives.

Commitment to the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)

A global vision. A commitment to people and the planet.

The Wahibi Foundation recognises the importance of the United Nations 2030 Agenda and the Sustainable Development Goals as international references for building a fairer, more inclusive and environmentally responsible society.

Our institutional priorities relate especially to the goals concerning quality education, health and well-being, reduced inequalities, innovation, environmental sustainability and strengthened partnerships.

We believe that sustainable development depends on bringing together knowledge, social responsibility and cooperation.

Transparency and Accountability

Trust is the foundation of all lasting transformation.

The Wahibi Foundation recognises transparency, ethics and institutional responsibility as essential principles for building solid relationships with society, partners and supporters.

Our commitment is to strengthen governance practices, promote the responsible use of resources and make institutional information and accountability documents available, as they become available and in accordance with applicable legal obligations.
