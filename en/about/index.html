About Us

Wahibi is a foundation committed to transformation.

Our Essence

The Wahibi Foundation was born from the conviction that transforming realities begins with recognising the value of every life, every culture and every community.

We believe that knowledge, science, culture and nature must walk together, creating opportunities where inequality exists and opening paths where once there seemed to be only limits.

We value the meeting between traditional knowledge and innovation, between the experience of communities and the possibilities offered by technology.

We are driven by a commitment to bring people together, strengthen partnerships and turn good ideas into initiatives capable of generating positive and lasting impact.

Our purpose is to contribute to a future in which development goes hand in hand with dignity, inclusion, responsibility and respect for life.

Mission

To promote human, social, cultural and environmental development through education, innovation, the appreciation of identities and the building of strategic partnerships.

To work to expand opportunities, strengthen communities and contribute to a fairer, more inclusive and sustainable society.

Vision

To be a leading institution in the promotion of human and sustainable development, recognised for its ability to connect people, knowledge and organisations around causes that transform lives.

To build bridges between cultures, territories and generations, contributing to a world where progress is truly at the service of people.

Our Values

  • Diversity and Respect

    We recognise the richness of different cultures, identities and forms of knowledge, promoting respect and inclusion in all our initiatives.

  • Care and Dignity

    We place human life, the well-being of communities and respect for nature at the heart of our decisions and actions.

  • Collaboration and Solidarity

    We believe that lasting change is built collectively, through dialogue, cooperation and responsible partnerships.

  • Ethics and Transparency

    We are guided by integrity, institutional responsibility and a commitment to the trust of partners, beneficiaries and society.

  • Memory and Identity

    We value the history, knowledge and traditions of peoples, recognising that preserving our roots is also a way of building the future.

What we do

Wahibi works across disciplines, connecting projects and people on multiple fronts:

  • Education and training grounded in diverse knowledge.
  • Culture and art as a language of memory and future.
  • Regenerative and green technologies, focused on inclusive solutions.
  • Biodiversity and territories, in defence of life in its fullness.
  • Public advocacy, through listening and community engagement.

Our initiatives take root in cities and traditional communities, in international networks and local initiatives — wherever there is fertile ground and a desire for change.

Governance

Under its Deed of Institution and Bylaws, the Foundation is led by three governing bodies, each with a 4-year term. These positions are unpaid.

Mazin Ali Al Wahibi

President of the Wahibi Foundation

At the helm of the Wahibi Foundation, Mazin Ali Al Wahibi represents a vision of leadership grounded in social responsibility, respect for different cultures and the conviction that development must create opportunities for all.

His institutional work is guided by the purpose of bringing people together, strengthening partnerships and encouraging initiatives that contribute to education, innovation, social inclusion and sustainable development.

Under his leadership, the Foundation seeks to build bridges between Brazil and the international community, bringing together knowledge, experience and effort around a common goal: turning opportunities into paths toward a more dignified, just and humane future.

  • Executive Board

    Chief Executive Director, Administrative and Financial Director and Technical Director, elected by the Board of Trustees. Manages the Foundation and carries out its programmes.

  • Board of Trustees

    5 full members and 1 alternate. Sets the Foundation’s general direction and approves activity plans, budget and accounts.

  • Fiscal Council

    3 full members. Oversees financial management and issues opinions on the accounts.
