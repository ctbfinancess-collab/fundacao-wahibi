Donations and Partnerships Policy

This policy sets out the Wahibi Foundation’s rules and commitments regarding the donations it receives and the partnerships it establishes with individuals, companies and institutions.

Draft document, subject to legal review before official publication.

1. Official donation channel

Donations are received exclusively via PIX, using the CNPJ key 60.512.994/0001-57, in the name of Fundação Wahibi para Inovação e Desenvolvimento, as shown on the How to Help page.

2. Donor safety

  • Before confirming payment, check that the beneficiary is Fundação Wahibi para Inovação e Desenvolvimento.
  • The Foundation does not request donations to personal accounts or through keys other than the official key.
  • If in doubt or if you suspect fraud, contact us before donating: contato@fundacaowahibi.org.

3. Use of funds

Funds received are allocated to the Foundation’s statutory purposes and focus areas. The amounts shown in the "Your Impact" section are illustrative examples of what each contribution can make possible.

Under the Bylaws, accepting donations with conditions attached requires the approval of the Board of Trustees, after consulting the Public Prosecutor’s Office.

4. Receipts

Proof of transfer is issued by the donor’s financial institution. For other requests, please contact us.

  • Refund policyTo be confirmed

5. Partnerships

Partnerships with companies and institutions must be aligned with the Foundation’s mission and values and are handled directly with our team through the form on the Become a Partner page.

Agreements and contracts are formalised through a specific instrument and approved by the Board of Trustees, as provided in the Bylaws.

6. Accountability

Accountability information will be made available on the Transparency and Governance page.

7. Personal data
