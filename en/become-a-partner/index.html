Become a Partner

There are many ways to contribute to the Wahibi Foundation and drive social transformation through innovation and technology. Across all our focus areas we use innovative solutions to create real and lasting impact. Choose the option that suits you best and be part of this change!

  • Monthly Donation

    By giving every month, you enable the Wahibi Foundation’s initiatives to reach more and more people across Brazil.

    • Continuous, predictable support
    • Sustainability for long-term projects
    • Regular impact reports

    Impact on Education

    A monthly donation of R$ 50 gives a child access to technology courses for one month, opening doors to a digital future.

    Become a monthly donor

  • One-time Donation

    A one-time donation is a direct way to support the Wahibi Foundation’s work financially and help expand its reach.

    • Flexible amount and timing
    • Ideal for occasional giving
    • Accountability on how funds are used

    Impact on the Environment

    A donation of R$ 200 can fund a solar energy kit for a family in an isolated community, promoting sustainability and inclusion.

    Make a one-time donation

  • Corporate Partnership

    Your company can develop a range of social impact, innovation and inclusion initiatives with the support of the Wahibi Foundation.

    • Tailored social responsibility projects
    • Employee volunteering engagement
    • Strengthening your brand with social purpose
    Start a partnership

  • Child Sponsorship

    Help increase the number of daycare places and improve the quality of life of children supported by partner organisations.

    • Follow the child’s development
    • Regular reports on the impact of your donation
    • Opportunity to visit partner institutions

    Impact on Social Inclusion

    With R$ 100 a month, you ensure a child has access to education and digital inclusion in one of our partner daycare centres.

    Sponsor a child
