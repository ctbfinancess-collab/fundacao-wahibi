Focus Areas

  • Education
  • Social Development
  • Innovation and Technology
  • Culture and Identity
  • Environment and Sustainability
  • Entrepreneurship and Income Generation

We work in an integrated way across complementary fronts, always focused on real and lasting impact.

  • Education

    Knowledge opens paths and transforms futures.

    We promote access to education, training and talent development, valuing learning as an instrument of inclusion, autonomy and social transformation.

  • Social Development

    Inclusion, dignity and opportunity for all.

    We work to strengthen communities, reduce inequality and expand opportunities, with special attention to people in vulnerable situations.

  • Innovation and Technology

    Technology and knowledge in service of humanity.

    We encourage innovative solutions, research and technological initiatives capable of tackling social challenges and contributing to more inclusive and sustainable development.

  • Culture and Identity

    Preserving our roots is building the future.

    We value cultural diversity, artistic expression, traditional knowledge and the memory of communities, recognising culture as an instrument of identity, belonging and transformation.

  • Environment and Sustainability

    Caring for the planet is caring for future generations.

    We support initiatives focused on environmental preservation, education for sustainability and the responsible use of natural resources, promoting balance between people and nature.

  • Health and Well-being

    Caring for lives transforms realities.

    We advocate for access to information, prevention and the promotion of physical and emotional health, encouraging initiatives that contribute to the quality of life and well-being of communities.

  • Entrepreneurship and Income Generation

    Turning talent into opportunity.

    We encourage entrepreneurship, professional training and the development of productive initiatives that strengthen financial autonomy and contribute to the prosperity of communities.
