Your project can transform lives. Shall we build this transformation together?

At the Wahibi Foundation, we believe great transformations begin with good ideas and people committed to making a difference.

If you represent an organisation, community, educational institution or company, or have an initiative capable of generating positive impact, we want to hear your proposal.

We are open to dialogue and to building partnerships in areas such as education, social development, science and technology, culture, sustainability, health and the creation of opportunities.

Bring your project to the Wahibi Foundation. Together, we can connect knowledge, mobilise partners and turn ideas into initiatives that contribute to a fairer, more inclusive and sustainable future.
